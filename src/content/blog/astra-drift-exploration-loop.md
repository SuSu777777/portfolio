**Astra Drift is a fictional Unity game concept**, not a released or playable project. The idea is to make space exploration feel rewarding without relying on a huge map or a long list of collectibles.

## Start with one clear loop

The player receives a signal from an unknown object, plots a route through a small solar system, travels there, scans it, and returns with a new piece of information. Each trip should answer one question and raise another. That gives exploration a purpose while leaving room for surprise.

I would prototype the loop with only one planet, three points of interest, and a single ship. If navigating that small space is not satisfying, adding more planets will only hide the problem.

## Make movement readable

An orbital map can become confusing quickly. The first version would use a simple two-dimensional route view: the current position, the destination, travel time, and one visible hazard. The player should be able to explain why a route is safe or risky before committing to it.

At a destination, scanning should reveal a visual clue rather than just another number. A strange orbit, a damaged satellite, or a repeating signal can tell a short story with very little text.

## A useful prototype test

Give someone a build with no instructions. Watch whether they can find a signal, choose a route, and understand what they discovered. The test is not whether they finish quickly. It is whether they know what they are trying to do and want to take one more trip.

If I develop this concept further, that small loop is where I would begin.
